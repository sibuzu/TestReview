Use case: illustration-story
Three equal square colorful anime educational scenes in one horizontal strip, explaining plant breeding goals versus propagation and genetic engineering. Precise scientific wording and comparisons will be HTML; no model-drawn DNA diagrams, gene counts, cellular pathways or microscopic processes.
Panel 1: A kind adult female plant breeder in a modest sun hat and practical field clothes studies several green crop test plants of modest differing forms in a research greenhouse, holding a completely blank record clipboard and thoughtfully comparing them. This represents observing candidate plants, NOT visual proof that every difference is inherited. No magical mutation, colored glow, mutant monster, pesticides or radiation.
Panel 2: A nursery worker tends trays of similar small rooted plant cuttings beside a healthy mother plant in a greenhouse. Cut stems and potted cuttings make the everyday idea of multiplying an existing plant clear. No seeds or flowers presented as the source, no claim every plant in every situation is genetically identical, no DNA graphics, no numbers that learners must count.
Panel 3: An adult female research scientist with modest white lab coat and protective glasses, in a clean agricultural biology laboratory, observes a tiny plant in a closed clear culture container and a simple unlettered laboratory screen. Only a conceptual research setting for introducing a new heritable trait, NOT an exact protocol and NOT an injection turning a mature plant instantly into a new color. No needles, colored gene arrows, DNA spirals, magic or marked successes.
Style: rich detailed cute colorful anime storybook, clear plants and warm expressive adult faces, bright daylight greens and gold, appropriate fully clothed lab and nursery workers. Clear at 180px square.
Layout: exactly three equal square scenes side by side, thin vertical separators, no header/footer or speech balloons.
Absolutely no words, fake writing, letter-like marks, labels, numbers, dates, diagrams, arrows, logos, watermark or flags anywhere. Clipboard pages, containers, trays and screen completely unlettered.
