Use case: illustration-story
Exactly three equal square richly colorful cute anime educational scenes in one horizontal strip for reading comprehension about a baseball article's TWO simultaneous conditions. This is a modern classroom exercise, NOT a recreation of a real match or any real player's intentions. Actual names, team scores, quotation distinctions and answer are native HTML only.
Panel 1: A Taiwanese teen boy and girl at a bright classroom reading table study a completely unlettered article on a tablet beside a plain baseball and glove. An adult female teacher gently helps them focus on the reading. No athlete portrait, team logo, uniform flag, scoreboard, real stadium, or named match image. The props only establish a baseball article's topic.
Panel 2: The teacher holds two plain empty cards side by side, one teal and one warm orange, while both students thoughtfully compare BOTH cards together. These are abstract classroom reminders of two separate conditions that must both be kept; no numerals, words, checkmarks, crossmarks, AND symbols, score marks or actual team color identities. Do not drop or hide one card, do not show automatic successful tournament qualification.
Panel 3: The students calmly compare the blank article tablet with a separate completely blank comment card while discussing evidence and interpretation. Thoughtful, respectful classroom expressions. No angry social media, abusive messages, conspiracy scene, exhausted athlete, intentional strikeout, cold blue muscles, hormone diagram or mind-reading imagery. The lesson distinguishes what the text states from unproven motive claims.
Style: rich detailed warm colorful cute anime storybook, expressive kind faces, modest fully clothed modern school students and teacher, cozy reading classroom, clear at 180px square.
Layout: exactly three equal square scenes side by side, thin neutral vertical separators, no extra subpanels, title/footer/captions or speech bubbles.
No text, letters, numbers, words, pseudo-writing, dates, scores, arrows, graphs, maps, logos, flags, badges, watermark or readable app interfaces anywhere; tablet screens, books and cards entirely unlettered.
